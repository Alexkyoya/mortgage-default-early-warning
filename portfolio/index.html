01 · The signal

Trouble shows up month by month

Among loans that later default, the share that is behind on payments climbs from 1.9% to 27.5% over the first year. Among loans that keep paying, it stays near 0.3%. A recurrent network can read that trend. Static origination data can't.

03 · Experiments & limits

What I tested, and where it breaks

Each experiment changed one setting and was run with three random seeds.

6 months of history is enoughPR-AUC 0.177 with 6 months vs. 0.179 with 12. Even 3 months scores 0.169, against 0.029 with no history.
GRU ties LSTMROC-AUC 0.902 vs. 0.904. With only 12 steps, the LSTM's extra gate adds nothing.
No class weightsWeights don't improve the ranking, and they inflate predicted probabilities up to 35× the true default rate.
Holds up out of timeTrained on 2015–16 and tested on 2017 Q1: 54.2% recall at 5%, vs. 22.1% for logistic regression.
Blind to sudden shocksROC-AUC drops to 0.78 in Florida and 0.80 in Texas after the 2017 hurricanes. Only 1.3% of missed defaults had any late payment.
